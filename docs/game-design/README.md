# Wyram game design

Wyram is a voxel sandbox about dwarves inhabiting a dangerous fantasy world left by populations of mythic colossi. Exploration feeds the player's ambitions to build, fight, invent and practice magic. The game supplies inspiration and systems that players can combine into creations of their own.

These documents formalize the direction agreed in the world-design discussion. They describe intended gameplay; implementation readiness is recorded separately in the roadmap. Illustrative names, materials and release examples remain proposals unless explicitly identified as settled direction.

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [World vision](world-vision.md) | World identity, scale, colossi, dwarven cultures and connected frontiers. |
| [Resources and creation](resources-and-creation.md) | Branching progression, material roles, bone balance and player-driven exploration. |
| [Delivery roadmap](roadmap.md) | Working foundation gate, initial surface delivery and recurring frontier expansions. |

The world vision and resource principles guide content decisions. The roadmap orders delivery without defining every future recipe, species or biome. Technical contracts remain in [architecture](../architecture.md), [plugin authoring](../plugins.md), [world generation](../world-generation.md) and the [plugin framework plan](../plugin-framework-plan.md). The [character gameplay plan](../gameplay-plan.md) and [performance plan](../performance-plan.md) retain their own implementation and validation work.

When a release changes a settled principle, update the relevant design document explicitly. Record its selected content, new public capabilities and acceptance evidence in the roadmap. Keep proposed content distinguishable from implemented behavior.
